Our story

The road here

a short version of a long story

My PhD journey has been a four-year road of growth, sacrifice, and perseverance. It was not easy. There were long nights, difficult moments, and seasons where the finish line felt far away, but it has been deeply worth it.

Along the way, I was carried by the support of family, friends, colleagues, mentors, and everyone who believed in me, encouraged me, and stood by me. Your prayers, calls, messages, patience, and practical help made more difference than words can fully express.

This celebration is my way of saying thank you. This achievement is not mine alone; it is ours.

“The work of the scholar is to think carefully and at length, and to remember those who carried him there.”
